Working with Vectors


1 Introduction

A vector is a sequence of values of the same type, and it is the most basic data structure in R. Most of what you will do in R, from normalising counts to filtering genes, is done with vectors. At the end of this lab you should be able to:

  • create vectors and calculate with them, element by element,
  • predict what happens when vectors of different lengths are combined (recycling),
  • select elements by position, by name and by condition,
  • modify, summarise, count and sort vectors,
  • generate sequences, repeated values, sample names and random samples,
  • store categories as factors and control their order.
